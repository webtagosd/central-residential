Our process

Six steps, start to finish.

Why it matters

A smooth process is essential for an enjoyable building project.

We make good communication a priority, so you feel up to date and informed on your project. We follow a six-step process on all our projects, so clients know where they are at and what to expect.

01

Initial enquiry

We discuss your requirements over email or phone, and then arrange a suitable time to make a site visit.

02

Site visit & design

At the site visit we will talk through the design and specifications of the project.

03

Scope of works & quoting

Once we have a design and specifications in place, CRB will create a detailed Scope of Works that outlines all tasks to be undertaken. Based off this, we will also provide a quote.

04

Upon quote acceptance

Once the quote is accepted, we will create a Master Builders contract to suit the project, and a timeline Gantt chart for the carpentry, trades and services. The timeline will outline a flow path for the project and indicative start to finish dates.

05

Construction

We manage CRB-supplied trades and materials, the on-site carpentry, health and safety, client expectations, and on-site inspections and quality control throughout.

06

Hand-over & completion

Upon completion, we will provide all necessary documentation for CRB-supplied trades, materials and carpentry. We can also help with the CCC process.
